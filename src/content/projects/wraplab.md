A full vinyl wrap costs $3,000 to $6,000, and most people choose it from a two-inch swatch. Wraplab shows the film on the whole car instead, in real light, in the browser.

## What it does

- **444 real films** from 3M, Avery Dennison, KPMF, Inozetek, and others. Every swatch carries the exact product code your installer needs.
- **Finishes that behave like film:** gloss, satin, and matte; metallic flake, pearl, chrome, and colour-shift.
- **Wrap part of the car.** Roof, mirror caps, trim, badges, wheels, and window tint each take their own film.
- **Compare and share.** A split view for two films, and share links whose previews name the film.

It is free to use. A one-time Pro unlock adds 4K exports, livery upload, saved designs, and every future vehicle.

The first vehicle is the Model 3. Wraplab is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Tesla.
